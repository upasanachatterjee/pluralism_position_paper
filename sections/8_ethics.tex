\section*{Ethics Statement}
\label{sec:ethics}

Deciding which perspectives a system should preserve is a value judgement, and our framework makes that judgement explicit without resolving it. It cannot be delegated to the retriever's relevance score, nor to how often a view appears in the corpus: frequency tracks who publishes, not who is affected. \citet{pmlr-v235-conitzer24a} argue that aggregation should reflect the values of the communities involved, and \citet{awad2018moral} show how sharply those values diverge. Any deployment that fixes such a procedure makes a normative choice on its users' behalf, and should say so.
